Support · DealPilot: Car Deal Checker

How can we help?

Answers to the common questions, and how to reach us.

Contact us

Email contact@universalaillc.com. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you tapped just before the problem.

In the app, "Report a problem" (Settings › About DealPilot) drafts a summary you can copy into your email. It includes the app version and the kind of deal, but never the VIN, prices, links or files. Please don't send us screenshots that show a seller's or your own personal details.

Private service contacts

Open Settings or Your data, then Private service contacts, to keep supplier, mechanic, transporter and broker details with a source and permission record. Nothing is sent automatically, and DealPilot does not check qualifications or consent. These contacts are separate from deal backups: use Export private contact backup, and review an imported contact backup before confirming it. Removing contacts or using Delete everything does not erase files you already exported or older device backups.

Export documents and shipment

First save both ports and the destination in the export cost worksheet. Open Decision, Report actions, then Export documents and shipment. Record requirements with a dated source; no universal document list is supplied. Requested, retained, submitted and owner-reported accepted entries are separate, and none establishes customs clearance. A requirement revision starts current progress unrecorded while keeping its history.

Record planned, changed, cancelled or owner-reported actual milestones. For a known time, enter the named time zone and its UTC offset in minutes for that date. Date-only entries have no exact instant; unknown times remain unknown. This tracker does not book transport or schedule reminders. A changed route or vehicle needs a new route record; earlier saved records stay in history. Private deal backups and the explicit shipment CSV include these details. Ordinary buyer reports omit them.

Getting started

Do I need an account?

No. DealPilot has no sign-in. Open the app and start a check, or open a worked example to see a finished one.

What are the worked examples?

Two finished deals with made-up (synthetic) numbers: an auction flip and a private-seller purchase. They show every part of a result, and they're free on every plan.

Where do the numbers come from?

From what you enter. DealPilot does the arithmetic: fees, tax, repairs, contingency, margin and the highest price that fits. It doesn't look up market values, find listings or sell history reports. The worked examples' fee schedule is synthetic too, so for a real auction, enter the buyer fees from its own terms.

Understanding a result

Why does a cost say "Unknown" instead of $0?

Because you haven't entered it yet, and DealPilot never treats a missing cost as free. Fill in the line it names, or mark it "Not applicable" if it really doesn't apply.

Why is there no max bid or max price yet?

A ceiling needs every cost to be known, plus your budget (when buying) or your expected resale and target profit (when flipping). The result names what's missing.

Is the result a recommendation to buy?

No. "Meets your target under these assumptions" is about the money, given the numbers you entered. It isn't a judgment about the car. The checks section lists what to confirm (title, VIN, inspection, mileage and prices) before you buy.

Does DealPilot check recalls or vehicle history?

No. It links to NHTSA's free recall lookup for the VIN, and it suggests a vehicle history report from an approved provider. Keep track of what you confirm in the checks section.

Is this financial, legal or tax advice?

No. DealPilot is a calculator and a checklist. Check tax, title and fee rules with your state and with the seller or auction.

Using it on iPhone

The VIN scanner doesn't open or doesn't read

Allow the camera in Settings › DealPilot › Camera. Then hold the phone steady over the VIN barcode, which is usually on the label in the driver's door jamb, in good light. You can also choose a photo of the barcode or type the VIN.

My deadline reminder didn't appear

Allow notifications in Settings › DealPilot › Notifications. Reminders are for auction payment and pickup deadlines you've marked as confirmed. They arrive at 9:00 the day before and 8:00 on the day, local time.

How do I save or print a report?

Open the deal's report and use Share. You can save a PDF to Files, send it, or print it. Personal details are hidden unless you turn that off. Sharing and printing reports is part of DealPilot Pro; the report itself is free to read.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything works offline except decoding a VIN and subscribing, which need a connection.

Your deals and backups

Where are my deals stored?

Only on your device. We don't have a copy and can't recover them for you, so please keep a backup.

How do I back up and restore?

In Settings › Your data, use "Back up all deals" to save a backup file to Files or iCloud Drive. To restore, choose that file with "Import a deal file" in your saved deals. Photos and documents stay on the device and aren't part of a backup. Backing up, restoring and deleting are always free.

What happens if I delete the app?

Its deals are deleted with it, unless you restore your iPhone from a device backup or import a DealPilot backup file.

Can I share selected repair and inspection records with a buyer?

For a purchased or sold flip deal, open Decision, then Report actions, then Prepare buyer repair and inspection records. Choose the records, prepare the preview, open each original and review the contents before exporting. Nothing is selected automatically. The ZIP includes a summary and only your selected original files; unzip it before opening index.html. The originals are unchanged and may include personal details, costs, VINs or photo location metadata. File labels and entered dates do not verify completed work, payment or vehicle condition.

Can I see what changed in a deal?

Open Decision › Report actions › Deal change history. Search recorded changes, expand before/after fields, or export the private CSV. History starts with changes observed by the app and can retain old private values; it is not an authenticated or tamper-proof record. Long values and large changes can be shortened, and older entries are trimmed at the local size limit. Clear private change history removes the retained history without changing current prices, expenses, buyer records or report figures. Backups and copies elsewhere are separate. If saving the clear fails, older history may still be on disk: retry and wait for saved confirmation.

How do I delete everything?

Open Settings › Your data in DealPilot and choose "Delete everything on this device". This can't be undone.

Subscription

What's free and what's in Pro?

Free, always: the Check tab (the full math from a few numbers, nothing saved, no account), the worked examples, Learn, and exporting or erasing your data. DealPilot Pro saves your deals and adds Compare, the shareable PDF report, snapshot history, your own fee tables, reminders and the Money tab. Saving your first deal is where the free trial starts, for eligible customers.

How much is it?

The configured U.S. reference prices are $19.99 per month or $99.99 per year. A 7-day free trial may be offered to eligible customers. The app shows the current Apple storefront price and renewal terms before you subscribe; check the offer displayed for your Apple Account. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled according to the terms shown before purchase. Prices and eligibility may vary by storefront.

How do I cancel?

Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then DealPilot. You can also tap "Manage subscription" in DealPilot's Settings › Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

What happens to my deals if Pro ends?

Nothing is deleted. Your saved deals become read-only until Pro is back. You can still read, back up and delete all of them, and the Check tab and the worked examples stay free. Prepared and previously shared report snapshots remain readable and exportable on this device after Pro ends. New shareable reports require Pro. Saved report figures are retained until you delete them or the deal; storage limits still apply. Backups are needed to recover from device-data loss.

I paid but Pro isn't unlocked

Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase, then tap Restore Purchases in DealPilot's Settings › Subscription. Check Apple’s purchase history before purchasing again if a transaction was interrupted or pending. If access still does not return, email support with the app version and what happened; do not send your password or full card number.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds ourselves. Eligibility and approval are determined through Apple; see Apple’s refund instructions.
