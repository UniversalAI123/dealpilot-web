Privacy policy · Effective October 23, 2026

Your data, your choices

DealPilot has no ads or tracking. Optional accounts, vehicle history reports and licensed market lookups operate only when a dedicated server is configured. This policy covers the DealPilot iPhone app and the DealPilot web version. It lists everything that can leave your device, and when.

In short

  • Optional email-code sign-in, needed only for vehicle history reports and other account features; selected-deal cloud sync requires separate approval
  • A vehicle history report sends the VIN you entered, and nothing else, to VINData, an approved NMVTIS data provider, only when you tap Run
  • No ads
  • No advertising analytics
  • No tracking
  • Deals are stored on your device; approved sync also stores private cloud copies
  • No sale of personal information; service processing is described below

Purchases start with an anonymous RevenueCat app ID. If an approved subscription connection is enabled and you choose it, a random purchase identifier links the subscription to your account. Optional sign-in collects an email and account identifier; deliberate market searches send the fields described below.

Who we are

DealPilot is made by Universal AI LLC, a Maryland, USA company ("we"). Questions about this policy go to contact@universalaillc.com.

What stays on your device

Your entries are stored on your device by default. If you separately approve selected-deal sync, our Supabase service also stores those chosen records and supported files under your account. Authorized service operators can access server records for support, security and operation; these copies are not end-to-end encrypted against the operator.

On iPhone, content you explicitly share to DealPilot waits in a private inbox for up to 7 days, until you import or discard it. Pending shared text and links are excluded from backups. Expired items are removed when the inbox is opened. Screenshot text recognition and receipt reading run on the device; their readers do not retain the image. Confirmed expense entries and any receipt attachments you choose to retain are stored with the deal. Offer records, negotiation notes, resale checklists and edited listing drafts also remain with the deal on your device. Exporting a listing draft is a separate action you choose; the app does not post it to a listing site. Deposit terms, payee names and viewing or inspection plans also stay with your local deal. Appointment locations are entered by you; this feature does not access GPS. Generated reports omit the separate deposit-payee, appointment-location and appointment-note fields, while deal backups retain them. An appointment CSV you choose to export includes its saved locations and notes.

Local change history can retain earlier values after you edit a field. It is included in ordinary backups but omitted from analysis reports and buyer summaries. You can review, export or clear it under Decision › Report actions › Deal change history. Clearing history does not change current deal facts or delete copies you already exported. File contents and attachment fields are withheld from app-recorded history; your own notes or imported records can still mention files. A failed history-clear save is not confirmed deletion until storage retry succeeds.

Export cost worksheets stay with your local deal, including entered currencies and exchange rates, quote sources and dates, route costs, and saved earlier scenarios. They are included in private deal backups and local recovery. Standard analysis reports and buyer summaries omit the worksheet. A worksheet CSV you explicitly export includes its private quote details and assumptions. Editing current deal costs does not rewrite an earlier scenario; deleting a deal or using Delete everything removes its stored worksheets. Copies you already exported remain wherever you saved them.

Read details can retain a private reference identifying the listing-text, screenshot or VIN-barcode intake that produced them, its completion time and an optional file ID. This reference keeps no raw text, image bytes, filename or listing URL. You can inspect source details in the deal. A file's current metadata can identify its name; removed or expired source files are marked missing. Corrections keep the first known original reference; older missing metadata stays unknown. Read references do not authenticate a source. Private backups and snapshots preserve these references; ordinary shared reports omit their IDs. Default personal redaction also withholds source-reference fields and free-text links, which can contain private access tokens. Explicitly turning redaction off can expose those references; check the preview before sharing.

Buying criteria are private device preferences for make/model, model-year range, mileage and price/margin limits. They do not fetch listings. Existing local native recovery includes these settings, but ordinary deal backups, iCloud Drive deal backup files and reports do not. Delete everything removes them. A report's Read first card uses only that report's own recorded, redacted contents.

Seller progress and private next-action notes stay with your purchase plan, private backups, snapshots and existing iCloud Drive deal backups when available. Suggested charge locations from on-device text reading keep only the charge label, amount, local file reference and image coordinates with the private deal; no extra copy of the image or raw text is stored by this feature. Changing the charge or linked file invalidates the location. Ordinary shared reports omit these locations and the next action. Contract review generates questions locally from your recorded charges; Copy puts only those shown questions on your clipboard when you choose it. Nothing contacts a seller or sends a contract automatically.

Optional model recall research retains the selected U.S. model year/make/model, exact NHTSA source address, retrieval date and public campaign records locally. Private backups, snapshots and native recovery retain it; ordinary shared reports omit it. Removing the current reference does not rewrite older snapshots. Model campaigns do not establish whether an individual VIN is affected or has an unrepaired recall. Imported local records are not independently authenticated.

Private export invoice comparisons retain frozen estimates, invoice and credit references, amounts, cost allocations, currency conversion sources, completeness reviews and final gross sale records on your device. They stay separate from cash-payment records. Ordinary shared reports omit this worksheet; an explicit private invoice CSV includes its financial sources, corrections and historical file IDs. Missing or expired supporting files do not erase earlier owner-entered financial transcriptions. The app does not independently verify invoices or payments.

Private export document and shipment records retain the captured route and vehicle facts, owner-entered requirements and source references, document progress, linked attachment IDs, dates, time zones and event corrections. They stay in private deal backups, snapshots and local recovery; standard analysis reports and buyer packs omit them. An explicit shipment CSV includes these private details. Removing or expiring an Evidence file does not erase the recorded claim, but its link shows missing; the log keeps no copy of that file's contents or name. Previous routes and app-recorded changes can retain earlier entries. No document authenticity, acceptance, shipment or customs clearance is independently verified. Date-only entries do not invent an exact time, and this tracker does not schedule notifications. Delete its deal or use Delete everything to remove the stored records. Earlier exports and device backups can retain copies.

Your private service contact book keeps the details you enter, including names, businesses, roles, phone numbers, email addresses, service areas, notes and your permission/source records. It does not access the phone's address book or verify consent, qualifications or contact accuracy. Saving sends no message. Contacts have a separate private backup and restore flow; deal backups, reports, buyer packs and snapshots omit the book. On iPhone the local recovery copy also keeps contacts, including when there are no vehicle deals. Removing a contact updates the book and its next confirmed recovery copy. Delete everything removes the book and clears open contact drafts. Exported files and older device backups can retain copies.

The last ten screen or JavaScript error categories and their timestamps are kept only in the open app's memory. Error messages, exception details, rejection contents and stacks are not copied into diagnostic downloads. Nothing is sent automatically. Report a problem lets you download build and device facts with these categories, or clear the categories; Delete everything also clears them. Later errors can create new entries. These categories do not measure native crashes or reliably count separate incidents.

On iPhone, review-request timing and your on/off preference are stored locally and in the local recovery mirror: first-use time, recent days with a saved task and reserved request times/app versions. Your device's own backup settings may include the recovery mirror. These records contain no deal identifiers, prices, ratings or subscription status. Settings lets you turn requests off, and Delete everything removes this timing data. Apple controls its system review prompt; DealPilot cannot tell whether it appeared or whether you submitted a review.

  • Your deals: vehicle facts, prices, cost lines, checks, notes, comparables and the results DealPilot works out.
  • Up to 20 ordinary snapshots per deal, plus prepared/shared report snapshots and pinned snapshots retained until you delete them or the deal. Device storage limits still apply.
  • Files you choose to keep follow the period shown in their picker. Listing screenshots offer 7, 30 or 90 days (30 by default). Files uploaded under Evidence offer 30, 90 or 365 days, or until you remove them. Expired files are removed when DealPilot runs cleanup, including at startup or when its files view is opened; removal can wait until you next use the app.
  • Your settings, such as the theme and your usual numbers.
  • In the iPhone app, deadline reminders you turn on, and a second copy of your deals in the app's private storage so iOS doesn't clear them.
  • If you enable Local usage summary in Settings, up to 90 days of yes/no activity flags stay on this device: app use, complete checks, opening saved deals, viewing the paywall, observed trial/Pro access, and active purchase responses. No car details, identifiers, prices or exact times are kept. Nothing is sent automatically. You may export a summary yourself; turning it off deletes it. It is separate from pilot logs and ordinary deal backups, but the device’s own backup may include it. Delete everything removes it.
  • If you turn on pilot mode, a usage log of the newest 10,000 events. It stays on the device and is never sent.

Like any app's data, this can be included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup. That backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

What leaves your device, and when

The subscription check can run automatically. Account requests and selected-deal sync require your actions and approval; after starting sync, approved records can transfer while the app is open. The flows below describe other requests.

  • NHTSA vPIC, the U.S. government's VIN decoder

    Where vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov

    What The VIN, and nothing else.

    When Only when you tap Decode. NHTSA's own privacy policy applies to its service.

  • NHTSA’s public recall, complaint and crash-rating API

    Where api.nhtsa.gov

    What The model year and make/model (your selection, or the one the decoder returned). No VIN, deal or listing text.

    When Once after you decode a VIN, when the Safety card reads that model year’s recalls, owner complaints and crash rating; and when you use Model recall research.

  • fueleconomy.gov (the U.S. EPA and Department of Energy)

    Where www.fueleconomy.gov

    What The model year, make and model, and the menu entries that narrow it down (such as “Civic 4Dr”). No VIN, price, location or other deal data.

    When Only when DealPilot looks up the EPA fuel economy and annual fuel cost for a car you decoded — once per car, and again if you pick a different version.

  • Your own iCloud Drive (Apple), only while iCloud Drive is on for DealPilot

    What One backup file of your saved deals — the same file “Back up all deals” makes — in Files › iCloud Drive › DealPilot. Photos and documents are not in it.

    When After a saved deal changes, at most every 10 minutes. iOS syncs the file to your iCloud; DealPilot has no server copy. Turn it off in Settings › your name › iCloud.

  • Apple Maps (Apple), through iOS

    What The two places of a trip you ask about — the car’s location as you typed it and your starting place or, if you tap “Use my location”, one reading of where you are — to work out a driving distance for the transport estimate.

    When Only when you ask for the distance. iOS asks before DealPilot can use your location; the reading isn’t saved.

  • Official consumer, inspection and EV guidance links

    What A normal visit to the official provider’s page when you tap its link. Guidance links include no saved deal, VIN or inspection notes; reading the in-app guidance sends nothing.

  • NHTSA public U.S. model recall API

    Where api.nhtsa.gov

    What The model year and selected official make/model, plus normal network request data. No VIN, listing text, saved deal, photo or account token. Credentials and referrer information are omitted.

    When Only when you load official makes/models or request model campaigns. Opening a retained reference does not refresh it. NHTSA’s own privacy policy applies.

  • NHTSA recall lookup

    Where nhtsa.gov

    What The VIN, in the address of the page that opens.

    When Only when you tap the recall lookup link. It opens in your browser.

  • VINData, an Approved NMVTIS Data Provider (vehicle history reports)

    Where api.vindata.com, through our account server

    What The VIN you entered, and nothing else. Your account’s report credit or included report is recorded on our server.

    When Only when you tap Run after the consent list, and when you choose Run again. The facts stay on your device for 90 days with a link to the VINData-branded report.

  • Listing links you save

    What Nothing. DealPilot never fetches them.

    When A saved link opens in your browser only when you tap it.

  • Apple's App Store and RevenueCat (iPhone app only)

    Where api.revenuecat.com

    What A random app ID, initially anonymous; your App Store purchase and subscription records; and device details such as the app version, iOS version and your App Store country and currency. An explicitly approved account connection uses a server-generated random purchase ID linked to your DealPilot account. It does not send your email, deals, VINs, photos or files to RevenueCat.

    When When the app checks or restores your subscription, when you subscribe, and after you approve an enabled account connection.

    Why To confirm your subscription and unlock DealPilot Pro. We can see subscription statistics in RevenueCat's dashboard. An approved connection lets our account service verify subscription access before an eligible paid lookup. Apple handles payment; we do not receive your card details or Apple Account credentials.

  • Things you share yourself

    What A report, a deal file, a backup, a CSV, seller questions or selected buyer records. Analysis reports hide personal details by default. Buyer-record archives include the original files you select, unchanged; review them for personal information, prices, VINs and photo location metadata before sharing.

    When Only when you share or save one, to the place you choose.

  • Apple billing-help pages

    What A normal page visit to support.apple.com, only when you tap an Apple billing-help link. DealPilot does not attach saved vehicle records or card details.

    When Your browser opens Apple's page. Apple's own privacy practices apply to that visit.

  • Email you send us

    What Whatever you write. "Report a problem" drafts a summary for you to copy or send from your own email app. It leaves out the VIN, prices, links and files. A billing-help draft includes the issue category, app/build version and purchase-check state. Opening or copying a draft sends nothing; you choose what to add and send. If you send it, your sender address and message reach our support mailbox.

    Why Only to reply to you and fix the problem.

Camera, photos and notifications

In a build with optional scanner support, entering your own private Wi-Fi OBD adapter address and confirming the parked vehicle starts fixed read-only requests over your local Wi-Fi. No code-clearing or ECU-write command is offered. Readings stay on the device unless you save them and separately approve syncing that deal. Physical adapter compatibility must be checked; this does not provide Bluetooth scanner or manufacturer-specific ECU coverage.

Optional saved-search push notifications require separate iPhone permission and an enabled Apple push service. Registration sends an Apple device token and environment to our account service. Notification text contains no VIN, prices, profit figures or location. Apple and network conditions affect delivery; attempted sends are not proof of receipt. Signing out stops notification registration on that device. The licensed discovery feed and Apple push service are currently not activated.

  • Camera: used only when you tap Scan, to read the VIN barcode. The image is read on your iPhone. Nothing is recorded or sent.
  • Photos and screenshots: you pick them with the system photo picker, so DealPilot sees only what you choose and never asks for access to your library. When DealPilot reads text from a screenshot in the iPhone app, it does so on your iPhone. Text reading itself does not upload the image. A supported file you deliberately include in selected-deal sync can be uploaded after separate approval.
  • Notifications: deadline reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Permission is asked only when you turn one on. A reminder shows the deal's name and the recorded deadline or follow-up date. It does not contact a seller or book an appointment.

What we don't do

  • Pilot sign-in is optional and does not start sync. Selected-deal sync needs separate approval.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers. DealPilot never asks to track you.
  • No advertising analytics or separate crash-reporting SDK. RevenueCat processes subscription records for functionality and purchase analytics.
  • No cookies.
  • We do not sell personal information, share it for targeted advertising or use data brokers. The service providers described here process data to run enabled features.
  • Local-only deals are not visible to us. Selected records approved for cloud sync are accessible to authorized service operators as described above.

The web version

The web version runs in your browser and saves your deals in that browser's storage. It works offline after the first visit. VIN decodes go from your browser straight to NHTSA, as in the app. There are no purchases on the web.

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub receives standard request data when the pages load, such as your IP address, your browser version and the page requested. Your deals are not part of it. GitHub's privacy statement covers that data.

Your choices

  • Delete everything: the "Your data" page in DealPilot (in the iPhone app, Settings › Your data) shows what is stored and has "Delete everything on this device". In the iPhone app, this also removes the second copy, shared files, pending shared listings and scheduled reminders. Deleting the app removes its data too, unless you restore a device backup.
  • Back up: you can download a backup of your deals. This does not remove any cloud copy you separately approved for sync.
  • Subscription: manage or cancel it in iOS Settings, under your name and then Subscriptions. Apple handles refunds.
  • Questions or requests: email contact@universalaillc.com. Your local-only vehicle records stay on your device; selected sync records also have private server copies. If you contact us, support can read your sender address and the message you choose to send. We can also help with questions about the subscription records RevenueCat keeps for this app, including an account connection you approved where that feature is enabled.

Children

DealPilot is made for adults buying or reselling vehicles. It isn't directed to children under 13.

Security

Your deals are protected by your device's own security, such as your passcode and iOS data protection. Every network request DealPilot makes uses HTTPS.

Optional accounts, vehicle history reports, sync and market comparisons

Requesting a sign-in code sends your email to the DealPilot account server and Supabase Auth. Entering that code starts a session kept in app memory; reopening requires another sign-in. The account API is hosted at iswogafkugrpcxgvtpvf.supabase.co. Authentication email is delivered through Resend. You can create an account with any email address you control; the account exists to tie report purchases and included reports to you so they can be re-opened. You can delete it in Settings › Account › Delete account; a fresh code confirms the deletion, which removes the account, its purchase link and its report and usage records on our server (it does not cancel an Apple subscription).

The account server limits code requests, verification attempts and session refreshes across its running instances. Its short-lived security counters use keyed digests of the email or refresh token, an action, a count and a window end; they do not store the raw email, token, code or IP address. Subject counters expire after one minute and are removed during requests or by a cleanup scheduled every five minutes while the database runs. Cleanup may wait if the project is paused. The global counter has no person identifier. This cleanup job keeps seven days of its operational history. Supabase service logs and backups have separate retention.

A market lookup sends the authenticated account ID and the requested model year, make/model/trim, ZIP and radius to our server. MarketCheck receives the vehicle search and location, not your complete deal, photos, attachments or Supabase token. Our server retains quota and request identifiers to prevent duplicate provider calls. Returned listings remain asking-price evidence. Saving selected comparisons requires review and permission under the provider contract. When retention is permitted, up to ten lookup results are held in app memory for up to 15 minutes so reopening does not need another provider call. Signing out, restarting or deleting local data clears that temporary cache.

A vehicle history report sends the VIN you entered, and nothing else, to our server and to VINData (VIN Data Products LLC, an Approved NMVTIS Data Provider) only when you tap Run after the consent list. It does not send your deal, photos, attachments or location. VINData returns title and brand records, junk, salvage and total-loss records, odometer readings, theft and lien records, and a link to its branded report. The facts are shown in the app for 90 days and kept only on your device (inside the app’s own storage, which your iPhone’s backup includes); after 90 days they are removed and only “checked on” remains until you run the report again. Our server keeps the request identifier and the credit or included report it used, so a report that was paid for can be re-opened and so no report is charged twice. Only when licensed storage is allowed, private completed results may be recoverable for 24 hours. Provider records and backups have separate retention. History coverage can be incomplete and does not verify a clean title or safe condition; the NMVTIS Consumer Access Product Disclaimer is shown with every report.

Optional saved searches store the chosen vehicle, ZIP, radius, seller type, price/mileage limits and daily-check approval privately in your account. Enabled daily checks send those criteria to MarketCheck and use your lookup allowance. Licensed search results may be retained for up to 30 days, subject to database maintenance and separate backups. Deleting a search removes its stored results. Push alerts are not connected. Quote requests open a reviewed draft in your own email app; that app handles sending and replies. Generated drafts omit private budgets and profit targets, and include the VIN only when selected. DealPilot does not infer that opening a draft delivered it.

Where subscription connection is enabled, your explicit approval asks the iPhone SDK to identify and restore your existing purchase. It does not buy a subscription or start a vehicle-data lookup. Our server stores your account-to-purchase ID mapping and a digest that prevents one vendor customer from receiving duplicate account allowances. It reads subscription status directly from RevenueCat rather than trusting a client Pro flag; full receipts and vendor response bodies are not stored by this flow. Test subscriptions and free trials do not unlock production data calls. Signing out disconnects the loaded purchase identity on this device and can require restoring Pro again; it does not cancel your Apple subscription. Account deletion removes the application mapping, while Apple/RevenueCat purchase retention is separate.

Signing in alone does not synchronize deals or files. In a build with Device sync, you may separately select deals and approve uploading their records, notes, entered city/state/ZIP, financial details and supported photos/documents to private Supabase storage. These records are linked to your account. The current sync limits are 8 MB per file and 50 MB per account. Timed-retention files and licensed MarketCheck comparison records pause sync pending appropriate cloud retention support/permission. Checks run while the app is open and visible after you start or resume sync; conflicting edits require review. Pausing or signing out stops future checks, while work already in progress may finish. Removing local data or deselecting a deal does not delete its cloud copy. Account deletion also removes the account-owned private sync files when that service is enabled. Signing out or deleting local data clears the loaded local account session. Deleting local data does not delete a Supabase account or server-side quota records; where the account service is connected and deletion is enabled, Settings → Account → Delete account requires a fresh email code and explicit confirmation. Confirmed deletion removes the Auth account and its account-owned workspaces, memberships, request receipts, licensed result copies, saved searches, purchase-identity mappings and account-keyed usage records. Aggregate daily cost counters contain no account ID and may remain. Device deals and attachments remain until you delete them separately. This does not cancel an Apple subscription. If deletion cannot be confirmed or email delivery is unavailable, use the support contact in this policy. Supabase, Resend, Apple and RevenueCat may retain service logs, purchase records or backups under their own policies; deleting an account does not promise immediate erasure of those separate copies. Support messages remain in our business mailbox while needed to answer requests or keep necessary business records; contact us to request deletion.
