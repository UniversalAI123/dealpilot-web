Privacy policy · Effective October 14, 2026

Your deals stay on your device

DealPilot has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. This policy covers the DealPilot iPhone app and the DealPilot web version. It lists everything that can leave your device, and when.

In short

  • No account
  • No ads
  • No analytics
  • No tracking
  • Deals stored on your device
  • Nothing sold or shared

The only data collected is anonymous purchase history for the subscription, handled by Apple and RevenueCat. It isn't linked to who you are.

Who we are

DealPilot is made by Universal AI LLC, a Maryland, USA company ("we"). Questions about this policy go to contact@universalaillc.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored only on your device. We have no server copy of it and can't see it.

On iPhone, content you explicitly share to DealPilot waits in a private inbox for up to 7 days, until you import or discard it. Pending shared text and links are excluded from backups. Expired items are removed when the inbox is opened. Screenshot text recognition and receipt reading run on the device; their readers do not retain the image. Confirmed expense entries and any receipt attachments you choose to retain are stored with the deal. Offer records, negotiation notes, resale checklists and edited listing drafts also remain with the deal on your device. Exporting a listing draft is a separate action you choose; the app does not post it to a listing site. Deposit terms, payee names and viewing or inspection plans also stay with your local deal. Appointment locations are entered by you; this feature does not access GPS. Generated reports omit the separate deposit-payee, appointment-location and appointment-note fields, while deal backups retain them. An appointment CSV you choose to export includes its saved locations and notes.

Local change history can retain earlier values after you edit a field. It is included in ordinary backups but omitted from analysis reports and buyer summaries. You can review, export or clear it under Decision › Report actions › Deal change history. Clearing history does not change current deal facts or delete copies you already exported. File contents and attachment fields are withheld from app-recorded history; your own notes or imported records can still mention files. A failed history-clear save is not confirmed deletion until storage retry succeeds.

Export cost worksheets stay with your local deal, including entered currencies and exchange rates, quote sources and dates, route costs, and saved earlier scenarios. They are included in private deal backups and local recovery. Standard analysis reports and buyer summaries omit the worksheet. A worksheet CSV you explicitly export includes its private quote details and assumptions. Editing current deal costs does not rewrite an earlier scenario; deleting a deal or using Delete everything removes its stored worksheets. Copies you already exported remain wherever you saved them.

Read details can retain a private reference identifying the listing-text, screenshot or VIN-barcode intake that produced them, its completion time and an optional file ID. This reference keeps no raw text, image bytes, filename or listing URL. You can inspect source details in the deal. A file's current metadata can identify its name; removed or expired source files are marked missing. Corrections keep the first known original reference; older missing metadata stays unknown. Read references do not authenticate a source. Private backups and snapshots preserve these references; ordinary shared reports omit their IDs. Default personal redaction also withholds source-reference fields and free-text links, which can contain private access tokens. Explicitly turning redaction off can expose those references; check the preview before sharing.

Optional model recall research retains the selected U.S. model year/make/model, exact NHTSA source address, retrieval date and public campaign records locally. Private backups, snapshots and native recovery retain it; ordinary shared reports omit it. Removing the current reference does not rewrite older snapshots. Model campaigns do not establish whether an individual VIN is affected or has an unrepaired recall. Imported local records are not independently authenticated.

Private export invoice comparisons retain frozen estimates, invoice and credit references, amounts, cost allocations, currency conversion sources, completeness reviews and final gross sale records on your device. They stay separate from cash-payment records. Ordinary shared reports omit this worksheet; an explicit private invoice CSV includes its financial sources, corrections and historical file IDs. Missing or expired supporting files do not erase earlier owner-entered financial transcriptions. The app does not independently verify invoices or payments.

Private export document and shipment records retain the captured route and vehicle facts, owner-entered requirements and source references, document progress, linked attachment IDs, dates, time zones and event corrections. They stay in private deal backups, snapshots and local recovery; standard analysis reports and buyer packs omit them. An explicit shipment CSV includes these private details. Removing or expiring an Evidence file does not erase the recorded claim, but its link shows missing; the log keeps no copy of that file's contents or name. Previous routes and app-recorded changes can retain earlier entries. No document authenticity, acceptance, shipment or customs clearance is independently verified. Date-only entries do not invent an exact time, and this tracker does not schedule notifications. Delete its deal or use Delete everything to remove the stored records. Earlier exports and device backups can retain copies.

Your private service contact book keeps the details you enter, including names, businesses, roles, phone numbers, email addresses, service areas, notes and your permission/source records. It does not access the phone's address book or verify consent, qualifications or contact accuracy. Saving sends no message. Contacts have a separate private backup and restore flow; deal backups, reports, buyer packs and snapshots omit the book. On iPhone the local recovery copy also keeps contacts, including when there are no vehicle deals. Removing a contact updates the book and its next confirmed recovery copy. Delete everything removes the book and clears open contact drafts. Exported files and older device backups can retain copies.

The last ten screen or JavaScript error categories and their timestamps are kept only in the open app's memory. Error messages, exception details, rejection contents and stacks are not copied into diagnostic downloads. Nothing is sent automatically. Report a problem lets you download build and device facts with these categories, or clear the categories; Delete everything also clears them. Later errors can create new entries. These categories do not measure native crashes or reliably count separate incidents.

On iPhone, review-request timing and your on/off preference are stored locally and in the local recovery mirror: first-use time, recent days with a saved task and reserved request times/app versions. Your device's own backup settings may include the recovery mirror. These records contain no deal identifiers, prices, ratings or subscription status. Settings lets you turn requests off, and Delete everything removes this timing data. Apple controls its system review prompt; DealPilot cannot tell whether it appeared or whether you submitted a review.

  • Your deals: vehicle facts, prices, cost lines, checks, notes, comparables and the results DealPilot works out.
  • Up to 20 ordinary snapshots per deal, plus prepared/shared report snapshots and pinned snapshots retained until you delete them or the deal. Device storage limits still apply.
  • Files you choose to keep follow the period shown in their picker. Listing screenshots offer 7, 30 or 90 days (30 by default). Files uploaded under Evidence offer 30, 90 or 365 days, or until you remove them. Expired files are removed when DealPilot runs cleanup, including at startup or when its files view is opened; removal can wait until you next use the app.
  • Your settings, such as the theme and your usual numbers.
  • In the iPhone app, deadline reminders you turn on, and a second copy of your deals in the app's private storage so iOS doesn't clear them.
  • If you turn on pilot mode, a usage log of the newest 10,000 events. It stays on the device and is never sent.

Like any app's data, this can be included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup. That backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

What leaves your device, and when

Nothing leaves without you doing something first, except the subscription check described below.

  • NHTSA vPIC, the U.S. government's VIN decoder

    Where vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov

    What The VIN, and nothing else.

    When Only when you tap Decode. NHTSA's own privacy policy applies to its service.

  • NHTSA’s public recall, complaint and crash-rating API

    Where api.nhtsa.gov

    What The model year and make/model (your selection, or the one the decoder returned). No VIN, deal or listing text.

    When Once after you decode a VIN, when the Safety card reads that model year’s recalls, owner complaints and crash rating; and when you use Model recall research.

  • fueleconomy.gov (the U.S. EPA and Department of Energy)

    Where www.fueleconomy.gov

    What The model year, make and model, and the menu entries that narrow it down (such as “Civic 4Dr”). No VIN, price, location or other deal data.

    When Only when DealPilot looks up the EPA fuel economy and annual fuel cost for a car you decoded — once per car, and again if you pick a different version.

  • Your own iCloud Drive (Apple), only while iCloud Drive is on for DealPilot

    What One backup file of your saved deals — the same file “Back up all deals” makes — in Files › iCloud Drive › DealPilot. Photos and documents are not in it.

    When After a saved deal changes, at most every 10 minutes. iOS syncs the file to your iCloud; DealPilot has no server copy. Turn it off in Settings › your name › iCloud.

  • Apple Maps (Apple), through iOS

    What The two places of a trip you ask about — the car’s location as you typed it and your starting place or, if you tap “Use my location”, one reading of where you are — to work out a driving distance for the transport estimate.

    When Only when you ask for the distance. iOS asks before DealPilot can use your location; the reading isn’t saved.

  • Official consumer, inspection and EV guidance links

    What A normal visit to the official provider’s page when you tap its link. Guidance links include no saved deal, VIN or inspection notes; reading the in-app guidance sends nothing.

  • NHTSA public U.S. model recall API

    Where api.nhtsa.gov

    What The model year and selected official make/model, plus normal network request data. No VIN, listing text, saved deal, photo or account token. Credentials and referrer information are omitted.

    When Only when you load official makes/models or request model campaigns. Opening a retained reference does not refresh it. NHTSA’s own privacy policy applies.

  • NHTSA recall lookup

    Where nhtsa.gov

    What The VIN, in the address of the page that opens.

    When Only when you tap the recall lookup link. It opens in your browser.

  • Listing links you save

    What Nothing. DealPilot never fetches them.

    When A saved link opens in your browser only when you tap it.

  • Apple's App Store and RevenueCat (iPhone app only)

    Where api.revenuecat.com

    What A random, anonymous app ID; your App Store purchase and subscription records; and device details such as the app version, iOS version and your App Store country and currency. Never your deals, VINs, photos or files.

    When When the app checks or restores your subscription, and when you subscribe.

    Why To confirm your subscription and unlock DealPilot Pro. We can see subscription statistics in RevenueCat's dashboard, such as trials started and renewals, but nothing that identifies you. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

  • Things you share yourself

    What A report, a deal file, a backup, a CSV, seller questions or selected buyer records. Analysis reports hide personal details by default. Buyer-record archives include the original files you select, unchanged; review them for personal information, prices, VINs and photo location metadata before sharing.

    When Only when you share or save one, to the place you choose.

  • Apple billing-help pages

    What A normal page visit to support.apple.com, only when you tap an Apple billing-help link. DealPilot does not attach saved vehicle records or card details.

    When Your browser opens Apple's page. Apple's own privacy practices apply to that visit.

  • Email you send us

    What Whatever you write. "Report a problem" drafts a summary for you to copy or send from your own email app. It leaves out the VIN, prices, links and files. A billing-help draft includes the issue category, app/build version and purchase-check state. Opening or copying a draft sends nothing; you choose what to add and send. If you send it, your sender address and message reach our support mailbox.

    Why Only to reply to you and fix the problem.

Camera, photos and notifications

  • Camera: used only when you tap Scan, to read the VIN barcode. The image is read on your iPhone. Nothing is recorded or sent.
  • Photos and screenshots: you pick them with the system photo picker, so DealPilot sees only what you choose and never asks for access to your library. When DealPilot reads text from a screenshot in the iPhone app, it does so on your iPhone. The image is not uploaded.
  • Notifications: deadline reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Permission is asked only when you turn one on. A reminder shows the deal's name and the recorded deadline or follow-up date. It does not contact a seller or book an appointment.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers. DealPilot never asks to track you.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No cookies.
  • We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't use data brokers.
  • No one at DealPilot can see your deals.

The web version

The web version runs in your browser and saves your deals in that browser's storage. It works offline after the first visit. VIN decodes go from your browser straight to NHTSA, as in the app. There are no purchases on the web.

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub receives standard request data when the pages load, such as your IP address, your browser version and the page requested. Your deals are not part of it. GitHub's privacy statement covers that data.

Your choices

  • Delete everything: the "Your data" page in DealPilot (in the iPhone app, Settings › Your data) shows what is stored and has "Delete everything on this device". In the iPhone app, this also removes the second copy, shared files, pending shared listings and scheduled reminders. Deleting the app removes its data too, unless you restore a device backup.
  • Back up: you can download a backup of your deals at any time. We keep no copy.
  • Subscription: manage or cancel it in iOS Settings, under your name and then Subscriptions. Apple handles refunds.
  • Questions or requests: email contact@universalaillc.com. Your vehicle records stay on your device. If you contact us, support can read your sender address and the message you choose to send. We can also help with questions about the anonymous subscription records RevenueCat keeps for this app.

Children

DealPilot is made for adults buying or reselling vehicles. It isn't directed to children under 13.

Security

Your deals are protected by your device's own security, such as your passcode and iOS data protection. Every network request DealPilot makes uses HTTPS.

Changes to this policy

If what DealPilot does with data changes, we'll update this page and its effective date before the change ships. The in-app "Your data" page always lists what the version you have can send.
